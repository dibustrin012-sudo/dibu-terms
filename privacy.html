Dibustring Clip Studio — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dibustring Clip Studio handles information when you use the Windows desktop application.

1. Information processed

The application may process video files, audio, subtitles, captions, titles, descriptions, tags, and other project information that you choose to open, create, or edit.

2. Local video processing

Video and project files are intended to be processed locally on your computer. Do not assume that every feature operates offline: connected services, updates, or optional integrations may communicate with external providers.

3. Third-party platforms

If you choose to connect or use a supported third-party platform, information may be shared with that provider as needed to perform the requested action. This may include account authorization data, video content, captions, titles, descriptions, tags, or publishing settings.

TikTok, YouTube, and other providers process information under their own privacy policies and terms. Review those policies before connecting an account.

4. Data storage and sharing

The application publisher does not operate a central video-storage service as part of the described local workflow. Project files and exports may remain on your computer. Temporary files may be created during analysis, transcription, preview, or rendering.

We do not claim that no information is ever transmitted. Transmission depends on the features, integrations, and external services you choose to use.

5. Permissions and account security

Only authorize account connections that you understand and intend to use. Keep your passwords, access tokens, and authentication codes private. You can review and revoke third-party application access through the relevant provider's account settings.

6. Retention and deletion

Files saved locally can generally be managed or deleted by you from your computer. Information sent to third-party platforms is subject to their retention and deletion practices. Temporary files may need to be removed separately if they remain after a task.

7. Children's privacy

The application is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. Users must comply with applicable age requirements and the rules of any connected platform.

8. Security

Use an updated operating system and obtain the application and its updates from sources you trust. No software or method of transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when application features or integrations change. The effective date above identifies the latest revision.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application publisher through the support channel provided with Dibustring Clip Studio.
